How it works

Two layers on every square plus the Surge. Rotate shapes with R / right-click, hover to preview, click to cast.

Zones — timed movement control

  • Bog (slow + pull), Gale (push), Mire (slow, initiative and range drain), Veils (shield vs Surges). They last 2 rounds and hit anyone inside, including you.
  • One zone per square. Opposed (Bog ↔ Gale) cancel each other. The same zone recast levels up and refreshes. Anything else replaces.
  • Push / pull move you 1 square from / to the patch centre at the start of your turn (push into a prop = collision damage).

Ground — permanent

  • Lava (Lava Field): burns if you end your turn on it (I = 1, II = 2). Stays until changed: recast = hotter, Bog cools it to Rubble, Gale fans it, Raise Ground cleanses it.
  • Attunement: squares start neutral. A fired Surge repaints what it hit; a fading zone repaints its squares. Zones and Surges are +1 on their own aspect's squares, −1 on squares of the aspect that beats them (Force > Nature > Dark > Force). Elevated, Rubble and Voidscar squares can't be attuned.
  • Elevated halts Surges and gives +1 range; debris collapses it to Rubble.

Surge — damage, then ground

  • Charges (blocks movement), fires at the end of the opponent's next turn: value ±1 by attunement − Veil shield. Props take 2 and shatter. Then the squares it hit are repainted in its aspect.

Simplified in this build

  • You carry 7 skills; real monsters carry a Surge + one utility. Wolf: Gloom Surge, Mire, Shade Veil.

# T18 · Specify Medicines and Arrays

**Archived (2026-11-21):** replaced by T58 (materials, pills and forged pieces); arrays do not exist, by the frame of [vision.md](../../vision.md). The text below is the state before it.

**Reshape 2026-11-13:** the High Ground Array (a start square as Mountain) and the Warding Array (less Surge damage) have lost their object ([overworld.md §6.8](../../overworld.md#68-arrays-artifacts-for-places)); the user named arrays as a possible form for squares that act once (Potential Extensions).
**Status:** Partly drafted — medicine and battle-array contracts/classification prioritized for the Battle gate; recipe and non-combat Array work stays open afterward.
**Prerequisites:** Relevant T07-D/T10 effect contracts for battle definitions, not completed healing tests or the T16 catalogue. T16 supplies recipes/acquisition later; T19 consumes resolved combat effects/costs.
**Sources:** Overworld [overworld.md §6.6](../../overworld.md#66-pills--medicines), [overworld.md §6.8](../../overworld.md#68-arrays-artifacts-for-places), [overworld.md §7.5](../../overworld.md#75-materials-seeds-and-grades)–[overworld.md §7.6](../../overworld.md#76-item-schema).
- **Battle deliverable:** concrete medicine/array/battle-prep effects, item classification, consumption/durability, placement and source-specific timing feed T19 and T13. **Meta deliverable:** recipes, purchase/crafting supply and Internal World/business-protection Array content follow after the gate.
- Determine Medicine effects, recipes, item kind and distinction from pills. Protection/healing/board effects need their own source, consumption and timing contracts in the shared data model; monster-effect contracts do not automatically approve items.
- Which Arrays are consumable, and which reusable? Define durability, placement and battle effects.
- Confirm pre-battle Arrays as a limited exception to a neutral/point-symmetric start; review the three listed battle Array drafts and separately define the claimed Internal World/business-protection scope; no additional Array record is currently supplied.
- *New:* the battle-prep pills Swift (+1 Pace point next battle; converted 2026-11-15 from +1 initiative) and Ward (first-hit immunity next battle) in [overworld.md §7.5](../../overworld.md#75-materials-seeds-and-grades) are neither medicines nor arrays; decide their item treatment, timing and effect contract. “Immunity” sits against the damage-modifier rules (reduction by named sources, no generic immunity; [battle.md §6.1](../../battle.md#61-damage), [skills.md §6](../../skills.md#6-passives); the earlier cite of a protection model in Skills §4 is stale, §4 no longer holds one); Warding Array (“first Surge damage −1”) and Spirit Shield (“next Surge damage is 0”) use different wording again. Also reconcile “pre-battle modifiers (pills, arrays)” in the Battle request ([overworld.md §8](../../overworld.md#8-technical-notes)) with the loadout categories.
- **Footing Array** (“one start square cannot be pushed or pulled onto/off”) is the only array draft with a live effect: check it against the displacement of monsters and props ([battle.md §6.4](../../battle.md#64-displacement)) and against Dragon Vein ownership before it counts as a battle input.
- **Done when:** Medicine/Array item kinds, effects, recipes, consumption and timing contracts are specified in the shared model, and the Swift/Ward pill classification is decided.

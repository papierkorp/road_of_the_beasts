# T64 · The Three Ways and the Day Without AP

**Closed:** replaced by T65 before its 'Done when' was met ([decisions.md](../../../decisions.md)).
**State before closing:** Closed 2026-11-22: the day, the dungeon, the forge and the cauldron were replaced before the Ways were played (T65); what is still to confirm about the Ways moved there. Before that: Decided (user, 2026-11-22; [decisions.md](../../../decisions.md)), written into the rule files and **built** ([godot/README.md](../../../../godot/README.md), "The hub game"; what was chosen while building: [implementation.md](../../implementation.md) I121–I132). Not played by hand.
**Prerequisites:** the user plays the three Ways.
**Replaces in part:** T54 (day AP), T55 (wild area, harvest), T57 (the systems), T58 (weapons, tiers).

**Decided:** three Ways that shut each other out (Artificer: board · Formation: tamer skills · Alchemist: monsters, with the branches body and kit); a day is one dungeon run or one attack on the void, no day AP, no wild area, no harvest; the dungeon gives monster XP and the reward of the Way (beast parts · Insight · plants); catching in the dungeon, Seal Ground by the means of the Way; a generated, mirrored battleground unless the Artificer lays parts (dungeon) or brings the own one (void); no weapons; the game is won by defeating the Sovereign beast; no bloodlines, grades or Kin gifts; no passive against pushes, holding a square is a self buff; plants without a tier, known plant by plant; skill slots develop by options of the skill's kind; stat points from pills are to be tried.

**Settled since (user, 2026-11-22):** the eight Kin traits stay for now, Unmoored too; a part may lie on any square of a dungeon board, without limits to start with; the vows are written anew and the nodes of the Artificer and the Alchemist are drafted ([overworld.md §5.3](../../../overworld.md#53-the-way)). The user asked for the build without further questions: what was decided on the way stands in [godot/README.md](../../../../godot/README.md) and [implementation.md](../../implementation.md).

**To confirm after play (chosen while writing and building):**

- The names of the Ways and their branches; the tamer skills of the earlier Way of the Beast Lord stand with the Formation.
- The Artificer has no tamer slots; the Alchemist fills them with pills only.
- Beast parts have no tier, like plants.
- The breakthrough choice of a monster: one of Breadth, Depth, Lore, Reshape.
- The numbers: parts carried (2 and 1 more per stage), 1 Insight per run for the Formation, stat points from a pill, the nodes of the trees, the list of vows.
- Whether the three Ways weigh the same at a trial.

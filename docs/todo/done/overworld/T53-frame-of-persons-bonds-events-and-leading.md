The details wait in T52. The text below is the draft as it was taken.
**Prerequisites:** none.
**Not part of it:** the insights and tamer skills by root; both belong to the progression of the player ([overworld.md §6](../../../overworld.md#6-progression)).

## 1. Person and Group

- **One card for every person,** all of it visible: stage; 1–3 roots, each a kind and a grade; fame; 2–4 traits; group and position, or none; bond to the player; hired or not; current wish.
- **Roots:** the kind says what work fits, the grade how fast and how far the person cultivates. Few roots: fast and high but narrow; many: slow and low but broad. The grade is potential, as rarity is for a monster, and one pill with a ritual raises it.
- **Traits:** drawn from one pool of good, bad and two-sided ones; one line each.
- **Two pillars:** a rogue cultivator has no group, is generated, comes and goes; a member has a group and a position. A key person is a member with an office, fixed traits and written wishes.
- **A group** shows: fame, the player's Standing, members by position, what it holds, its events and what sets them off.

## 2. Bond and Hiring

- **One ladder for persons and groups:** Stranger → Known → Trusted → Allied → Sworn; for a group it is Standing. A fulfilled wish raises it by one; a request of a group is its wish. Sworn never sinks. No gifts and no favor to spend.
- **A wish** comes from a pool (key persons: written), asks for what the game makes (a pill, a piece, a monster, a scripture, spirit stones, a deed) and grows with the worth of the person. Its price is shown before.
- **When a wish appears:** for anyone when the player asks; for a hired person at every turn of a season. Only an ignored wish of a hired person lowers the bond; whoever falls below Trusted leaves.
- **Hiring** is a state apart from the bond: possible from Trusted and only if the player's fame reaches the person's. Hiring a member harms the group by a shown amount. A Sworn member may stay in their group as an ally.
- **Luring away:** a rival's offer for a Trusted follower stands on the calendar; a wish fulfilled before the turn keeps the person. Allied holds while no wish is ignored. Lent equipment returns; what was spent on the person is lost.
- **What each rank gives** per pillar: the table of the talk (Trusted: a rogue can be hired, a member helps once; Allied: may be a steward, speaks for the player in the group; Sworn: stays for good).

## 3. World: Stages, Calendar, Events

- **The spine** is the stage: the trial of each breakthrough is the only required battle, and Sovereign ends the game.
- **Calendar:** day → season → year. All that recurs falls on the turn of a season: wishes of the hired, rogues arriving and leaving, offers of rivals, persons cultivating.
- **Events replace the ordered arcs.** An event has: what sets it off, its day on the calendar (shown ahead), its course (a battle on a battleground, an order, a choice) and its outcome (fame, Standing, goods, further events).
- **Two ways to set one off:** the state of the player (member of the school, fame, stage, an organisation) or the calendar of the world (a realm opens, the monster riot). What the calendar brings returns or waits; nothing single is lost by time alone.
- **Two sources:** written events (the six former arcs, the events of each group) and events from pools (wishes, rogues, realms).

## 4. Leading People

| Way | How | From |
|---|---|---|
| Alone | hired persons, each ordered directly | the start |
| A position | Standing Allied or Sworn with a group lends some of its members for orders | the start |
| An organisation of one's own | sect, gang or trading house: one schema, the kind sets which orders it does well and which group is its rival | Core Formation |

- **No fixed number of followers:** every hired person brings a wish per season; the player holds as many as they can serve.
- **Stewards:** a follower from Allied on leads a group. The player orders the steward; the steward brings one wish for the whole group.
- **Orders:** one list, reachable from anywhere. An order has a kind (a root) and a level (a stage). A person fits by root and counts by stage plus equipment; traits change it. The night decides and the outcome is shown before.

## 5. Fame and Goods

- **Fame replaces weight:** one value from 0 to 6 for persons, groups and the player; public deeds raise it, nothing lowers it. It opens hiring, offers and events. What groups do when the player passes them is an event set off by fame.
- **Pills change who a person is** (root grade, a trait removed, the stage). **Forged pieces change what a person can do:** a weapon or a tool per kind of root, counted on orders.

## What It Would Replace

Weight and its table, favor as points, the ordered arcs, Strength and Loyalty of the people, the house as the only way to lead people.

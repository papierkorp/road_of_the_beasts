- **Purpose:** every stat should support meaningful choices and monsters should differ through their kits and decisions. Earlier bot/kit observations motivate testing, not a universal verdict that Pace, Stamina or fragile bodies have no value.
- **Decided by the user:** the skill-delivery model and removed mechanics are owned by Battle §1.2/§4 and Skills §2–§4. Preserve those decisions; do not reopen the discarded universal Shove or standalone stat-zone alternatives.
- **Open (user):**
  - **A job for Pace and Stamina.** Since 2026-11-15 Pace also sets the Stamina recovery and the Movement stat is the Stamina pool; whether that gives both a job is unmeasured. Test touch control, leaving damage and turn timing with suitable kits and human decisions; existing bot results do not isolate their value. First-arrival-dependent vein damage and distance-moved rewards remain unadopted ideas, not missing approved mechanics.
  - **First-play feedback is decided:** more non-Strike displacement in kits, no Shove action for everyone; Dragon Veins remain standable and last touch owns; stat zones only as extras on other skills. Validate rather than reopen discarded alternatives. **Prototype exception:** Blaze Tiger's user `new_skill` is an experiment Arc Strike and its current kit does not meet the non-Strike displacement goal ([content.md §5](../../../content.md#5-the-nine-monsters)). Preserve the authored file; ask for the design decision later, do not silently restore Headbutt or approve this as a default.
  - **Battle length and candidates:** T13 owns the current evidence review and the links to S020–S031. One central vein versus three is next for human review; costs, repetition limits, one-shot survival, new skills/items and maps remain authorized experiments, not automatic adoption. No price/entry-proxy restart.
  - **Squares that act once and vanish** (Potential Extensions): **decided by the user on 2026-11-15: they exist as walkable props** (a trap acts when entered and is used up; [battle.md §4.3](../../../battle.md#43-the-square-ground-zone-and-occupant)/[battle.md §4.7](../../../battle.md#47-props)); the “no traps” decision of 2026-11-13 is lifted. Content: Spike Trap, Vortex Trap and the generator's button runes (T41, T07/T21).
  - **Stamina and a job for Pace:** Stamina as the rule with recovery by Pace (T03/T41) and Pace with blocking by walls and zones are untested by humans; bots cannot score blocking and do not budget Stamina.
  - **Dragon Veins:** count and squares for boards without a centre row; the owner's choice at a tie is decided and built for the human side; the owner mark in the view is built (tint, label, log line) and not yet seen by a human; props on a vein (T39).
  - **Removed-mechanic dependants:** Ancient's Strike effect (T06), Wardstone and other prop effects (T07), Spirit Shield (T20), High Ground/Warding Arrays (T18) and passive candidates (T11). Ancestor Tablet's current Mossbed aftermath is a working implementation, not the former Hallowed effect; validate or replace it rather than treating it as missing.

# T11 · Learn Lists, Passives and Kits

**Status:** Open — nine hand-made kits exist ([content.md §5](../../../content.md#5-the-nine-monsters)); no species has a learn list; one passive is in the game and no kit equips it.
**Prerequisites:** T10; T06 for Kin identity.
**Sources:** [skills.md §1](../../../skills.md#1-the-kit), [skills.md §6](../../../skills.md#6-passives), [content.md §5](../../../content.md#5-the-nine-monsters); [content.md §5](../../../content.md#5-the-nine-monsters).
- **Kits:** use current prototype Resources and the manual-experiment boundary in Skills §10, not historical bot rankings as identity rules. The user's earlier experiments (`asdf`, `new_skill`) were deleted on his instruction on 2026-11-15. Player-shaped kits need species learn lists (draft about 8 skills: 4 defaults + 1 per breakthrough, [monsters.md §5](../../../monsters.md#5-growth); against six slots see T05).
- **Kits of the build (2026-11-15, user instruction: six skills, balanced, for the multiplayer mode):** the nine monster files and [content.md §5](../../../content.md#5-the-nine-monsters) agree: every kit has a damaging Strike, a skill that moves others, a ground and a prop skill, and all but the Forge Golem a movement skill. The sixth skills are the agent's choice and unconfirmed one by one; the user's `new_skill` (Blaze Tiger) is deleted. Content `2026-11-15.5`; not played, not measured. The skills and their calculation are the next thing to rework (T02, T10).
- **Kit requirement (confirmed 2026-11-13; reworded 2026-11-15, reading to confirm):** [content.md §5](../../../content.md#5-the-nine-monsters) now reads “at least one skill that pushes or pulls”: since damage and push of a Strike are both optional and belong to the single skill, a Strike that pushes counts. With it every one of the nine kits meets the requirement (Gloom Wolf through Shove and Thorn Lash, Blaze Tiger through its Strikes). If the user meant displacement at a distance, the wording has to say so and Blaze Tiger and Gloom Wolf fall short again.
- **Kin access:** the earlier movement and skill lock-outs are gone ([monsters.md §3](../../../monsters.md#3-kins)); [skills.md §1](../../../skills.md#1-the-kit) no longer names Kin lock-outs (corrected 2026-11-15). A new access rule is open (with T06).
- **Passives that lower AP costs (user decisions 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15):** allowed, and no action ever costs less than 1 AP; that floor is read as the only fixed limit and stands in [skills.md §6](../../../skills.md#6-passives). The earlier draft limit of this list (“at most −1 AP, only while a named condition holds”) is not decided. The concrete passives, how much each lowers and under which condition are still to be written.
- **Other passives:** the candidates that still fit are listed in Skills §9 without numbers; Frenzy is unresolved.
- **Passive as identity:** [monsters.md §1](../../../monsters.md#1-species-and-individual) calls the passive the biggest personality driver and [overworld.md §7](../../../overworld.md#7-technical-notes) stores a “species passive”, while a passive is an ordinary kit slot (Skills §9) that no current kit uses. Decide whether a species has a fixed passive, then align the three texts.
- **Done when:** every species of the roster has a learn list and default kit, the passive list is decided, and Skills §9/§10 carry them.

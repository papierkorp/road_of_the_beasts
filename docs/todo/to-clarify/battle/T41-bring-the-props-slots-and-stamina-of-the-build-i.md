# T41 · Bring the Props, Slots and Stamina of the Build into the GDDs, or Drop Them

**Status:** Partly decided — on 2026-11-15 the user decided the rule questions, and they are written and built: traps exist as walkable props, props and zones may be on Dragon Veins, a solid prop gives cover, the round end has its prop step, Stamina is the rule, the prototype plays with six open slots. The prop sections of the Battle GDD were rewritten on the model of the build ([battle.md §4.7](../../../battle.md#47-props)). **Still open:** which content of the build's catalogue stays, the conflicts below, and the details of Stamina.
**Prerequisites:** the user's play of the build (T40/T13).

- **Written as working draft, content to confirm (D):** the catalogue of [content.md §3](../../../content.md#3-props) (Earth Wall, Thorn Idol, Ember Brazier, the Storm Drum acting at the end of the round, Spike Trap, Vortex Trap, the three experiment runes), the seven placing skills in [content.md §4](../../../content.md#4-skills) and the fifth kit slots in [content.md §5](../../../content.md#5-the-nine-monsters). The user has not said which of them stay.
- **Conflicts the catalogue still carries:**
  - **(a) Fair damage at range:** the Thorn Idol (1 damage to its eight neighbours when it is hit) can be set next to a monster and struck in the same turn: damage at a distance with no turn of the victim in between ([battle.md §1](../../../battle.md#1-goals-and-pillars) pillar 5 and [battle.md §6.1](../../../battle.md#61-damage) mark it as open). The Ember Brazier at the end of the round behaves like Blight.
  - **(b) Parked idea built:** “props or ground that damage neighbouring squares” (Potential Extensions, parked by the user 2026-11-12) exists as Ember Brazier and Thorn Idol.
  - **(c) Aura props:** archived in the build; Battle §4.10 names them as not in the catalogue, and §4.12 keeps “while it stands” possible for a prop. Decide whether they return (with T07).
  - **(d) No clock for created props:** what a skill lays on the board is temporary by the decision of 2026-11-12, but a prop a skill creates stays until it is destroyed. The user's sheet sketch of 2026-11-15 gives a prop an optional duration in rounds.
  - **(e) Strikes and walkable props:** a walkable prop has HP and is struck and pushed like any prop in the shape; whether striking a trap sets it off is unwritten.
  - **(f) Generator-only props:** the button runes cannot be created by skills (“board only”); they exist as a board switch, not as a rule (T07, T21).
- **Stamina (decided 2026-11-15: the rule; [battle.md §2.4](../../../battle.md#24-action-points-and-stamina), [monsters.md §2.1](../../../monsters.md#21-the-stats)):** the pool is the earlier Movement stat, a movement skill pays the movement cost of every square it enters, and the recovery is the whole number of Pace, at least 1. **Open details (D):** a movement skill keeps its AP cost (decided 2026-11-17, the switch is gone); `slow` and `haste` change the Stamina a movement skill costs per use (decided 2026-11-17); the user's sheet sketch points to a movement cost of the square instead; with T07); the range of 3 per skill and the pool sizes (T03); the reading that the recovery follows the Pace on the sheet. [S029](../../../simulation_log.md#s029--2026-11-14--stamina-at-1-per-square-last-touch-back-as-the-build-default), measured as a switch with a recovery of 1: with the bots the pool shows no clearer slope than Movement (they do not budget Stamina), +2 per round flattens it, and Stamina only (no AP) shortens battles to about 3.1 rounds.
- **Not built:** props that move by themselves, a Weight stat. A recovery stat is not planned: Pace does that job (user, 2026-11-15: few stats). The one-use “button” square is built as a board switch (2026-11-15, I64).
- **Push and pull for props (decided by the user and built, 2026-11-14; values are working drafts):** push and pull move props like monsters ([battle.md §6.4](../../../battle.md#64-displacement)): Strikes, landing pushes, Gong/Vortex Urn aftermaths and, at the round end, push/pull zones (a prop has no turn); `heavy` props stay; a blocked prop takes 1 and a monster in the way 1 (2 from a `spiky` prop). Readings the code chose (I63): zones move props at the round end before zones fade and before prop round-end operations; props rank after monsters; a walkable prop is also blocked by a monster; AI and view do not yet value or show moved props. Still open: whether a prop pushed onto a Dragon Vein, a trap or into another prop's square needs more rules, and whether props moved by zones should rather act at some other moment.

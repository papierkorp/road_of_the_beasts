# T07 · Ground and Zone Values

**Decided:** Roles (ground at a moment, zone while inside), independence of zone and ground, no healing or damage by zones, unlimited Mossbed entries are decided; ground, zones and props share one sheet format with those roles as a permission table (user, 2026-11-15; [battle.md §4.8](../../../battle.md#48-one-sheet-for-ground-zones-and-props)); every value is a working draft.
**Prerequisites:** play (T13/T40).
**Sources:** [battle.md §4.6](../../../battle.md#46-zones), [battle.md §4.5](../../../battle.md#45-ground), [battle.md §4.4](../../../battle.md#44-lifetimes).
- **Ranged damage is weak by design now:** hazards deal their level (1, at most 2), only Binding Vines 2. Check in play whether ground pressures enough or battles are decided by Strikes and Dragon Veins alone.
- **Damage zones as skills (user missed them in manual play, 2026-11-14):** by decision zones never deal damage ([battle.md §4.6](../../../battle.md#46-zones)); ranged damage comes only from ground. The draft list has damaging ground skills (Lava Field, Cinder Dive, Fire Trail, Thornbed, Gloom Rift and Coiling Rift with Blight; Snare 2 on leaving), but each hits for 1 (at most 2) at a later moment. Open for the user: is the gap that such damage is too weak or too slow, or that a zone-like timed area damage is wanted? The second reopens the zone role and the fair-damage rule ([battle.md §6.1](../../../battle.md#61-damage)); first check values and skills (this task, T10) before changing the role.
- **Binding Vines:** 2 on leaving, no levels, may be laid under a monster. Is a second cast a refresh only; does a blocked push count (no: no movement); is 2 right against HP 3.
- **Healing:** only Mossbed (1 on entering). Whether a ground that heals at the end of a turn is wanted (the earlier healing zone is archived).
- [ ] **“Button” squares (user idea; built as the board switch `map_buttons` on 2026-11-15, number field “Button pairs” in the draft screen; experiment content, no rule):** a square the map generator places that does something on the board once a monster collects it (enters it, also by a push), without damage. Built as walkable runes: Moss Rune (Mossbed on the 3×3 block), Bog Rune (Bog zone, 2 rounds, on the 8 squares around it, pulling toward the rune square), Wall Rune (Earth Wall on each free cardinal neighbour); each is used up. Decide the values here after play: which effects, the area size, whether it is used up, how many per map and where (point-symmetric, [battle.md §4.1](../../../battle.md#41-board-generation)); its form (a prop, a ground, its own thing) is the open question of T41 and the Potential Extensions row on squares that act once. Keep “no damage at range without a turn since the cast” ([battle.md §6.1](../../../battle.md#61-damage)).
- **Stat effects on a square:** only zones (and props that work while they stand: none in the catalogue). The archived Hearth, Umbra and Hallowed could return as zones if wanted.
- **Sheet vocabulary (2026-11-15):** the moments are fixed (no start of a turn or of a round). In the vocabulary but in no content: AP and turns-per-round changes on other monsters; the user named them as possible effects (a monster at 0 turns does nothing that round). Decide whether they ever enter content: a lost turn would be the harshest effect in battles of 3–5 rounds and has to stay avoidable. Under Stamina, decide whether `slow` / `haste` become a movement cost of the square (the user's sheet sketch gives ground and zones a movement cost of 1–3) or stay a change of range (with T41).
- **Levels:** hazards to II by laying again, zones to III by casting again; check whether levelling is used or only clutters the rules. With one use per turn (decided 2026-11-14) a monster levels its own ground or zone only in a later turn, with a second copy of the skill, or together with another monster, and all of it inside a 2-round clock.
- **Prop dependants:** Wardstone's former Surge reduction needs redefinition/removal; the Wardstone is archived together with the other aura props (T41; [content.md §3](../../../content.md#3-props) names them as not in the catalogue). Ancestor Tablet currently leaves Mossbed instead of Hallowed; validate or replace that working aftermath ([content.md §3](../../../content.md#3-props)), not the removed effect.
- **Done when:** the values are confirmed in play and written into Battle §4.6/§4.7 without the open marks.

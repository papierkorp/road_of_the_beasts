- **Displacement directions:** [battle.md §6.4](../../../battle.md#64-displacement) holds them as the build plays them: a Strike, a landing push and a prop move along the offset in eight directions; a push zone moves in its cardinal direction, a pull zone cardinally toward its anchor and not at all on an exact diagonal. The user has not confirmed the table.
- **Corner cases of a diagonal move:** between two blockers, into a board corner. Unwritten.
- **Who places first** before a battle ([battle.md §2.2](../../../battle.md#22-core-loop)).
- **A Draw:** what the result carries beyond “not won” (with T19).
- **Leaving and entering in one move:** a push off Binding Vines onto Thornbed fires both, the leaving effect first. Written; to confirm once played.

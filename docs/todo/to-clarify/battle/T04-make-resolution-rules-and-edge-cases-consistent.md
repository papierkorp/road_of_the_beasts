# T04 · Make Resolution Rules and Edge Cases Consistent

**Status:** Partly decided — the rules that survive the reshape are in [battle.md §2](../../../battle.md#2-modes-and-battle-flow); the questions about Surge scheduling, Mountains and the air layer are gone with them. On 2026-11-15 the user decided the round-end order and cover, and the text conflicts of Battle §2.4/§4.11/§6.4 were written out; the displacement directions and the measure of Reach are written as the build plays them and wait for confirmation.
**Prerequisites:** none; the points that concern props are settled together with T41.
**Sources:** [battle.md §2.2](../../../battle.md#22-core-loop), [battle.md §3](../../../battle.md#3-timing), [battle.md §4.7](../../../battle.md#47-props)–[battle.md §4.7](../../../battle.md#47-props), [battle.md §6.2](../../../battle.md#62-payload-resolution), [battle.md §6.4](../../../battle.md#64-displacement); [skills.md §3](../../../skills.md#3-what-a-skill-does-and-creates), [skills.md §5](../../../skills.md#5-movement-kinds); [Implementation](../../implementation.md).
- **Draw result:** both sides without monsters after one resolution is a Draw; what the BattleResult carries and what follows in an expedition is open (with T19/T23).
- **Late-round pressure:** removed from the multiplayer battle (user, 2026-11-18); the brake for modes without Dragon Veins is open in T39.
- **Dragon Vein tie (decided by the user and built, 2026-11-14, as in [battle.md §4.2](../../../battle.md#42-dragon-veins-surrounding-and-late-round-pressure)):** among equally near, equally strong enemies the owning side picks the target at the round end. Built: the round end waits for `choose_target` when the owning side is in `Battle.choice_sides` (the player in the battle scene); the AI and simulations pick by a fixed rule (user decision 2026-11-14): fewest HP, then highest Pace, then lowest max HP, then roster order. The preview is built (each option shows its HP after the round end, or “falls”, from a dry run on a copy; not seen by a human yet). Open: whether the choice set is wider than the current exact tie (the three criteria of Battle §4.2 already prefer more HP and more Pace, so the AI rule only separates units equal in HP and Pace).
- **Readings the code chose** (I01–I18, I51, I65–I77): the ones about Mountains, the air layer and Surges are void; confirm or correct the rest in their owner sections: walking direction (I01; Skills §8.2 now states cardinal steps for Step and Run, the user has not confirmed it), board-edge Strike legality (I02), Tail Sweep on multi-cell shapes (I04), total Pace (I11), late-round start rounds (I15), and the readings below.
- **Leaving and entering in one move:** a push off Binding Vines onto Thornbed fires both, the leaving effect first; confirm the order with the user once played.
- **Push and pull zones at cast (decided by the user, 2026-11-14: no; written into Battle §2.4 and §4.6):** a zone does not move anyone when it is cast; it pushes or pulls only at the end of the occupant's turn (S025: mean 2.41 → 3.41 rounds, defeats before the victim's own first turn 8 → 0). §6.1 “fair damage at range” holds again for zones. Remaining: the tests that pin the old behaviour (`TestHelpers.legacy_rules()`), the review lead on the `CREATE_ZONE` asymmetry (T13), and Battle §6.2 step 3, whose pull wording (“nearest the anchor first”) now applies only to what props do.
- **Round-end order (decided by the user, 2026-11-15; written into [battle.md §3](../../../battle.md#3-timing) and built):** first everything moves, then everything acts, then all clocks tick together: push and pull zones move props → props that push or pull (Storm Drum) → round-end ground → the other round-end lines of the props (Ember Brazier) → Dragon Vein and surround damage → Stamina recovery by the current Pace → zones lose a round, timed unit effects expire, ground laid in battle fades → late-round pressure → queue. Tests pin it (`test_at_the_round_end_everything_acts_before_the_clocks_tick`, `test_at_the_round_end_a_storm_drum_pushes_before_ground_and_props_act`, `test_lava_does_not_act_at_the_round_end_but_when_its_occupant_ends_a_turn_on_it`).
- **Pace ties (new, D):** on a grid of 0.1 with the tie going to the higher base Pace, a drain of one Pace point never passes a monster with a lower base: Siphon and Mire at level I only reorder monsters of equal base Pace. Decide whether that is wanted or whether drains count double, or the tie rule changes ([battle.md §2.3](../../../battle.md#23-pace-and-the-queue); with T06).
- **Displacement directions (written as the build plays them; to confirm):** [battle.md §6.4](../../../battle.md#64-displacement) now holds one table: a Strike that pushes, a landing push and a prop move along the offset in eight directions; a push zone moves in its cardinal direction and a pull zone cardinally toward its anchor, with no move on an exact diagonal (I67). Unwritten: the corner cases of a diagonal move (between two blockers, into a board corner).
- **Reach is measured two ways (new, K/D):** [skills.md §2](../../../skills.md#2-the-skill-card) says “within Reach (board distance from the user)” and [battle.md §4.1](../../../battle.md#41-board-generation) makes board distance Chebyshev; the build counts orthogonal steps (Manhattan, `BattleCasting.error`), so a diagonal anchor is out of Reach sooner than the GDD says (I72). Decide which one holds.
- **Zones and props (written 2026-11-15):** [battle.md §4.7](../../../battle.md#47-props), [battle.md §4.3](../../../battle.md#43-the-square-ground-zone-and-occupant) and [skills.md §3](../../../skills.md#3-what-a-skill-does-and-creates) now say the same: the stat effects of a zone do not apply to a prop, a push or pull zone moves it at the end of the round, and that step and the props' round-end lines stand in Battle §2.4.
- **Strikes and props (decided 2026-11-15):** a solid prop takes the hit and shields the square behind it ([battle.md §4.7](../../../battle.md#47-props), I65); a Strike without damage pushes a prop and does not damage it ([content.md §3](../../../content.md#3-props)).
- **Movement over squares (reading, new):** only walking acts on every square of its way; a straight line and a free path count for the landing square alone, for ground, Dragon Veins and traps alike (I66). Skills §3/§8.2 say so only for Leap, and [battle.md §4.2](../../../battle.md#42-dragon-veins-surrounding-and-late-round-pressure) gives a vein to whoever “walked onto or across it”. Step is “1 square, cardinal” in Skills §8.2 and “neighbouring square” in §11.
- **Done when:** each point has a rule in its owner section and the readings are confirmed or corrected.

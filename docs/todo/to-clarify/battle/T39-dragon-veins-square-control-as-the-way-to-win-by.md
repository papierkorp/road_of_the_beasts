# T39 · Dragon Veins: Square Control as the Way to Win by Defeat

**Status:** Partly decided *(user decisions 2026-11-13: three squares on the centre row, damage at the end of a round, surrounding, extra turns from round 2, late-round pressure only as a brake; ownership by last touch; all working drafts in [battle.md §4.2](../../../battle.md#42-dragon-veins-surrounding-and-late-round-pressure) / [battle.md §2.3](../../../battle.md#23-pace-and-the-queue), standard of the build; bot runs S013–S020; first human play exists, full validation open)*.
**Prerequisites:** play.
**Sources:** [battle.md §1](../../../battle.md#1-goals-and-pillars), [battle.md §4.1](../../../battle.md#41-board-generation), [battle.md §4.2](../../../battle.md#42-dragon-veins-surrounding-and-late-round-pressure), [battle.md §4.5](../../../battle.md#45-ground); [simulation_log.md](../../../simulation_log.md) S013, S017, S018, S024–S028.

- **User frame (2026-11-13):** the win condition stays “all enemy monsters defeated”; square control is the way there. The queue by Pace (then initiative) stays (no alternating activation), four free skill slots stay (six since 2026-11-14), monsters keep their variety. Rejected by the user: extra damage against a monster that cannot evade, armour by facing, alternating activation, healing or AP on these squares for the start.
- **Current comparison:** S024 and T13 compare one central vein with unchanged three-vein control. Lower passive damage and longer means come with wider tails; no causal stat-role or fairness conclusion follows. Three veins remain the normal 7×7 default; one vein has not been adopted. Earlier rejected/unselected candidates remain evidence in the log, not parallel active rules.
- **Vein rule (user decision 2026-11-14 after S028): last touch owns**, the GDD rule and the build default again; "vein held" stays a switch (`vein_touch` = false). [S028](../../../simulation_log.md#s028--2026-11-14--new-build-defaults-prop-rework-balanced-kit-draw-and-stamina): held veins run longer than the 3–5 target with the stronger bot and flatten Movement.
- **Props on a Dragon Vein (decided by the user, 2026-11-15: allowed):** a Dragon Vein is ordinary ground that nothing changes, so props and zones may be on it ([battle.md §4.2](../../../battle.md#42-dragon-veins-surrounding-and-late-round-pressure)); a solid prop on a vein keeps monsters off the square and is a way to deny it. Reading to confirm: board generation may start a mirrored prop pair on veins too (built; only the centre square stays free). Still unwritten: whether the AI should value blocking a vein.
- **Board size under test (user request 2026-11-15; switches, not rules):** 3v3 on 8×8 and 9×9 ([battle.md §4.1](../../../battle.md#41-board-generation)). An even board has no centre row: its veins lie in mirrored pairs on the two centre rows and their number is even, so no side can hold a majority by one. First indications (S030, T13): the number of veins moves the length more than the board does (8×8 with two veins is the longest row, with four the shortest and the richest in Draws); 9×9 with three veins reads 4.9 (greedy) and 5.5 (tactic) mean rounds against 4.5 and 5.1 on 7×7. **After the shape merge (S031)** the default board, 8×8 with four veins *(the confirmed default of the 8×8 switch)* and 9×9 with five veins all lie inside the length target under both bots (tactic 4.70 / 4.80 / 4.67 mean rounds; 44 / 41 / 32 of 160 battles over 5 rounds; 7 / 4 / 0 under 3), so the bots give no reason to prefer one board for the length; the larger boards have fewer very short battles and more Draws. Decide after play whether 3v3 keeps 7×7 and, for an even board, how many veins.
- **Measured alternatives, not adopted:** charged veins ([S026](../../../simulation_log.md#s026--2026-11-14--charged-dragon-veins-and-a-step-over-the-full-movement), [S027](../../../simulation_log.md#s027--2026-11-14--charged-dragon-veins-with-the-damage-at-the-end-of-the-round); the slope of Pace (then initiative) with `zone_cast_push` off, S026 early defeats removed by S027's end-of-round damage) and held veins (S028: longer than the target, flatter Movement). They remain switches, not parallel rules.
- **Open:** count and squares per mode (even boards have no centre row; the build places none on 1v1 and 2v2, three on the centre row of 9×9 as on 7×7, and 1v1 is the mode the player starts in, T13); the owner's choice at the last tie is decided and built for the human side; whether damage starts in round 1; a mark in the view for who is hit next (the owner is shown since 2026-11-14); 2×2 bodies are out of scope (T09). **Surrounding stays (decided by the user, 2026-11-14).**

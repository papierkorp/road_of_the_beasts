# T02 · Find Skill Prices

**Decided:** —
**Built/Measured:** **reset by the reshape of 2026-11-13.** No skill, ground, zone or prop has a price. The earlier construction-point sheets priced Surges, timers, Mountains and air coverage and were removed with that model.
**Prerequisites:** play of the reshaped build (T40, T13); a stable skill list (T10).
**Sources:** [skills.md §7](../../../skills.md#7-value-and-prices), [skills.md §2](../../../skills.md#2-the-skill-card), [skills.md §4](../../../skills.md#4-shapes), [content.md §4](../../../content.md#4-skills); [simulation_log.md](../../../simulation_log.md) S020–S031.
- **Earlier progression direction (S022; S023 new gameplay takes priority):** working start and upgrade paths are user-approved goals, not approved numeric starts. Keep stat/skill ledgers separate and validate joint action thresholds; calculator prices are not frozen. Current ZONE PUSH/PULL overrides include mixed Slow effects as in S021. Proxy AP 2 leaves authored cost-3 `new_skill`, `asdf`, `strike_thrust` unaffordable despite all units having an affordable active; next check starter kits and distinguish AP-3 unlocks from extra actions. No source budget fit checked or price adopted.
- **Principles that stand** (earlier user decisions): a skill is paid with AP and its use limit; no skill rarity; a complete kit at original AP; stats and skills priced apart.
- **Evidence boundary:** [S020–S024](../../../simulation_log.md#s024--2026-11-14--one-dragon-vein-count-control) compare rules, kits, action thresholds and maps, not equal-price components. Prices cannot repair a mechanical lack of meaningful choices; bot results do not prove that a stat or skill has no value. Review whole skills and combinations before deriving prices.
- **Open:** what a price is expressed in (AP only, or AP plus a kit budget); whether the 1-AP and 2-AP split of the build holds; the value of the two `experiment` effects (AP +1, one more turn) that the user wants rare and expensive; prices of created props.
- **Price parts added on 2026-11-14 (to price, nothing is priced):** the use limit is now a real part of a price (once per turn by default, `usage_limit`); a second copy of a skill in another slot buys a second use with a slot ([skills.md §1](../../../skills.md#1-the-kit)); slots 5 and 6 (T05).
- **AP values inside the price-free model (K/D):** since 2026-11-15 a Strike's AP stands on its card like every other cost; [skills.md §4](../../../skills.md#4-shapes) keeps draft AP for the ten shapes that had one (Jab 2 up to 5 for Arc +1, Whirl +1 and Cross +1) and “open” for Jab +2, Pincer +1 and Storm +1. Shove therefore costs the 1 AP of its card; the build charged it the 2 AP of the Jab shape in every run up to S030. Decide with the price form whether the per-shape values are the price list in the making or placeholders, and mark them alike in §5, §8.
- **Price parts added on 2026-11-15 (to price, nothing is priced):** an extension of a base shape; a square of a free shape (the arrangement is the player's, so the number of squares is what is left to price); damage and push of a Strike as two parts; Stamina as a second cost of movement, and the range of a movement skill.
- **First price list (2026-11-16, draft, not approved):** the user asked for a skill calculation like the monster calculation, with first prices from bot runs ([decisions.md](../../../decisions.md)). It is built as `godot/battle/defs/skill_calc.gd` (shown in the skill builder and the overview; `skill_impact.gd -- prices` lists every skill) and fitted in [S032](../../../simulation_log.md#s032--2026-11-16--what-the-parts-of-a-skill-are-worth-random-kits-of-generated-skills-a-first-price-list-and-its-check). Form chosen while building, to confirm: 10 points = 1 AP; zero point: a Jab with 2 damage and a push = 20 points; AP from 1 to 5.
- **What S032 leaves open:**
  - **(a) Placed skills below 1 AP:** every ground, zone and prop skill is worth less than 1 AP against the Jab, so the list cannot tell them apart by AP. Options: accept the floor; make them stronger (two uses a turn, more rounds, a second effect); a second cost beside AP (a kit budget in points); a cheaper zero point (then the Jab costs 3 AP).
  - **(b) AP above 2:** the bots rarely afford a skill of 3 AP; a linear price per AP overprices large shapes. A stepped price, or no skill above 3 AP?
  - **(c) Extra squares** of a shape have no measurable value for the bots, who do not aim an area. Price them by play, not by the runs.
  - **(d) Combinations** (a landing push with a hazard or a solid prop is worth most) are not in the price of a skill: leave them to kit building, or price them?
  - **(e) Not measured:** Stamina cost of a movement as a price part, the `experiment` self effects (AP +1, one more turn), slots 5 and 6, a second copy.
- **Direction of 2026-11-17 (being tried, T43):** Stamina as a second price of Strikes (S034: 2 Stamina on top of 3 AP removes a skill from play), and Strikes without damage (S035). With the second, damage and the Strike shapes need new prices; the fit of S032 has to be repeated on the rules that stay.
- **Form decided by the user (2026-11-17):** a calculator like the monster calculator; each of the three AP levels gets a range of points (less is always allowed). Open: the ranges, and the prices on the standard rules (build default: Strikes without damage). The existing skills are temporary and no measure for it.
- **`internal_calc_cost`** in the Resource files holds numbers of the removed sheets for older records and −1 for everything since: decide whether the field stays.
- **Done when:** the user has played, a price form is decided and written into Skills §5, and every skill of the game carries a price that play and runs support.

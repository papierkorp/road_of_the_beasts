# T05 · Complete Skill Improvements

**Status:** Partly decided — the development pattern is decided (2026-11-18, [skills.md §8](../../../skills.md#8-development)): levels 1–3 with level 1 as the base, one option per level from a list, the option vocabulary, Count for monster skills only, replacement keeps the level with options chosen anew. Slot plan decided on 2026-11-15 (multiplayer plays with six open; in the game single slots are upgrades). Still open: the options of every skill, slot unlocks, step mapping details, deferral, respec, the relearning pill economy, where the choice is made.
**Prerequisites:** T02 for prices; T10 for a stable skill list. **Battle first:** which developed states are legal on a test sheet (slot count, levels, what a development changes) comes before the meta part (pill economy, where the choice is made, day AP), which waits for the gate.
**Sources:** [skills.md §8](../../../skills.md#8-development); [monsters.md §4](../../../monsters.md#4-rarity)/[monsters.md §5](../../../monsters.md#5-growth); [overworld.md §5.5](../../../overworld.md#55-development-of-monster-skills).
- **Decided (user, 2026-11-08):** permanent slot development only at advancement steps, on one slot of one monster; replacement with a relearning pill keeps the slot's development; slot levels 1–3; never more damage, lower AP or more Reach.
- **S022 evidence boundary:** the damaging-Strike + Thornbed proxy (only without an existing ground BoardOp) preserves AP/damage and other fields; it is not an approved slot choice. Unchanged outcomes do not prove absent value. Validate alternative paths and joint stat/slot action thresholds; no lower-AP upgrade. Viable entry and useful alternatives are goals, not a numeric start or confirmed development option.
- **Slot plan (user decisions 2026-11-14 and 2026-11-15):** up to 6 slots; the multiplayer mode plays with all six open; in the game single slots are upgrades ([skills.md §1](../../../skills.md#1-the-kit)). **Decide for the game:** how many slots a monster starts with; what opens a further slot (an advancement step, a rarity, an item) and whether that competes with the slot-development steps of Skills §6; whether the stage table carries it. **Files that still say four (K):** [monsters.md §1](../../../monsters.md#1-species-and-individual) and [overworld.md §5.1](../../../overworld.md#51-stages) (“4 slots”); they wait for that decision. Skills §7, §9 and §10 were corrected on 2026-11-15 (one to six skills, six builder slots, the fifth kit column). A 6-slot kit also changes the “about 8 learnable skills” draft ([monsters.md §5](../../../monsters.md#5-growth)).
- **Epic+ “extra passive slot” (decided by the user, 2026-11-14: struck):** [monsters.md §4](../../../monsters.md#4-rarity) no longer grants one; rarity gives development steps and the maximum stage only. Remnants (K, T35): §5.1 still says rarity “buys slots”, §5.2 “unlock new rarity's slots/caps”, [battle.md §2.1](../../../battle.md#21-modes) points to “kit size and rarity exceptions” and Overworld §6.3 to “rarity slots”. The Epic+ second artifact slot of Overworld §6.3/§6.7 is a separate matter under T17.
- **Open on the skill card:** which parts a development may change (one more extension of a base shape or one more square of a free shape, zone rounds, range and what a movement passes over, a second thing created, `usage_limit` — the candidates of Skills §6); step-to-level mapping; step supply incl. Normal rarity; deferral; respec; passive-only slots; the relearning pill's economy (T16/T31); the Evolution Pillar's function.
- **Supply against capacity (audit arithmetic 2026-11-15 on legacy working values):** [monsters.md §5](../../../monsters.md#5-growth) has nine advancement rows with a slot-development step (steps 4, 7, 10 and 13–18). Normal ends at Core with 2 steps, Rare at Soul with 3; Epic reaches Immortal with 10–12 and Legendary Sovereign with 14–18, depending on whether the rows written “+1 slot-development step” (14, 15, 17, 18) also double for Epic and Legendary. Six slots with levels 1–3 hold 12 developments if level 1 is the starting state and 18 if it is the first development. Decide the mapping, and whether a spread of 2 / 3 / 10+ / 14+ fits “rarity is potential, not raw power” ([monsters.md §4](../../../monsters.md#4-rarity)).
- **Two ways to repeat a skill:** one more use per turn as a development (`usage_limit`), and the same skill in two slots, each copy with its own count ([skills.md §1](../../../skills.md#1-the-kit)). Decide whether both stay once slots 5 and 6 exist.
- **What a replaced slot keeps:** Skills §6 lets a slot keep “its development” when the skill is replaced, although the options are written for the equipped skill; [overworld.md §5.5](../../../overworld.md#55-development-of-monster-skills) refers to a “retained progress/upgrade reselection” that Skills §6 does not define. State what carries over: the level with options chosen anew, or nothing that belongs to the old skill.
- **Learn list against six slots:** 4 defaults + 1 per breakthrough give 8 learnable skills at Immortal and 6 for a Normal monster that ends at Core; with six slots that leaves little or no choice.
- **Done when:** these points are decided and Skills §6 lists the development options of every skill of the game.

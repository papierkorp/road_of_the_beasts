Favor and weight are numbers without a clear purpose: weight as a sum can hardly be kept low, and what groups do at high weight is not defined.

**Goal, four directions that partly exclude each other:**

| | People are | The player lives | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | a second team: few named ones, found, trained and equipped like monsters | attachment, students who grow | a second monster system beside the core |

The agent's leaning (draft, not agreed): C as the core, B only as its effect (a named person carries out one order at night).

**Goal questions for the user:**

1. Attachment or mass: does the player know single people by name, or are they a quantity?
2. Where does the decision lie: whom to take in, where to send them at night, whom to trust, what to give up for them?
3. Do they grow through the stages themselves, or are they fixed and better ones are fetched?
4. Can they be lost or turn against the player, or are they safe like a monster under contract?
5. Do key persons and the player's own people belong together: can a rival or a teacher become one's own?
6. How much may it cost: a depth of its own, or flat on purpose beside the battle as the core?

**Proposals of the agent that wait for the goal (drafts, not decided):**

- **Weight:** replace the sum by a shown stage: what the city believes of the player, on the scale of the six stages, never above the true stage, raised only by showing oneself at the end of an arc. A high shown stage opens doors (offers need Standing and a shown stage); a low one means being underestimated: whatever the city sends against the player is sized to the shown stage. Craft ranks, the house and equipment count toward nothing. Variants: a public win above the shown stage uncovers the player; no value at all; keep the sum.
- **Favor:** Standing alone, one value per group that deeds raise and harm lowers by an amount shown beforehand, with ranks from a hostile one to Sworn; no favor to spend and no grudge rule. Reason: the frame names "no points to spend" for the ladders of deeds, and a Standing that never falls lets a player befriend all in the end. Variants: Standing alone that never falls; keep Favor and Standing. This would replace the model chosen for T27.
- **People:** Strength and one trait, without Loyalty; the rest waits for the house, which is not part of the first slice.

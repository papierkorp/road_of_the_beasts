# T65 · The Wild, the One Clock and Drops Instead of Crafting

**Status:** Decided (user, 2026-11-22; [decisions.md](../../../decisions.md)), written into the rule files and **built** ([godot/README.md](../../../../godot/README.md), "The hub game"; what was chosen while building: [implementation.md](../../implementation.md) I133–I141). Not played by hand.
**Prerequisites:** the user plays the three Ways.
**Replaces:** T64 (the day as one deed, the dungeon), and in part T54 (days), T55 (the dungeon), T58 (materials, forge, cauldron).

**Decided:** a roguelike without an advantage for the next game (the pool of eggs still grows); the Sovereign beast stays the goal; the lifespan is the one clock and every deed costs a year of it; the wild shows a few battles and a new look costs a year; a wild battle won lifts monsters by one step, each monster defeated lifting one monster at its step or below, the player choosing where the rises do not reach; a monster rises into the next stage only by a battle won in the void; every battle starts at full HP; parts and pills drop ready from the wild, per Way; a boon at every breakthrough, a longer life among them.

**To confirm after play (chosen while writing and building):**

- **The numbers:** 10 to 14 years per stage; 1 year for a wild battle, a new look and an attack on the void; 3 battles per look; 1–2 up to 2–4 monsters per battle by stage; an egg hatches after 2 battles.
- **The drop:** one unit per monster of the battle (a part, a pill); the Formation gains 1 Insight from a battle against 2 or more. Pills are drawn evenly from all ten kinds, the strong ones (Tempering, Honing, Growth) included.
- **The look:** a battle won leaves it and the others stay; when all are won a new look comes for nothing; a battle lost waits.
- **A trial won costs no year;** a trial lost before its time does.
- **The void lifts every monster that fought,** whatever its step, not only those at High.
- **A rise not given** is lost with the next battle.
- **The boons:** Long Life (+3 years), Wide Hand (a tamer slot, a part more), Keen Eye (one more battle shown); one may be taken again.
- **The vows** that named the dungeon, XP or brewing were changed or dropped (14 are left); the nodes of the Alchemist were rewritten (Rich Find, Deep Pouch, Open Meridians).
- **Insight has too little to buy** once the Formation gains it in the wild. Two proposals wait for the user, neither is built:
  - **Awakenings** (every Way): Insight buys a passive for one own monster in the Monsters building, one per monster, from the gifts the Kins had before (Tireless, Surefoot, Brawler and the like). Wild and void monsters never have one, so the player reads no passive on an opponent. This follows the user (2026-11-22): a passive on the own monster is fine, having to check the opponents for passives was the burden.
  - **Levels for tamer skills** (Formation): levels 1–3 per skill, an option per level ([overworld.md §5.4](../../../overworld.md#54-tamer-skills)).
- **From T64, still open:** the names of the Ways and their branches; the Artificer without tamer slots; the breakthrough choice of a monster; parts laid per battle (2 and 1 more per stage); stat points from a pill; whether the three Ways weigh the same at a trial.

**Balance** (years per stage, the weight of the three Ways) is checked by the user by hand; no simulation is planned.

**Done when:** the user has played the three Ways and confirmed or changed the points above.
